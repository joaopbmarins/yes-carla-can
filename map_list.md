# CARLA Maps

The following tables list the available maps in CARLA, along with a summary of each.

> **Source:** [CARLA 0.9.15 – Maps and navigation](https://carla.readthedocs.io/en/0.9.15/core_map/)

## Non-Layered Maps

Non-layered maps have all layers present at all times — they cannot be toggled on or off.

|  Town  |                                                          Summary                                                          |
|:------:|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------:|

| Town03 | A larger, urban map with a roundabout and large junctions.                                                                |
| Town04 | A small town embedded in the mountains with a special "figure of 8" infinite highway.                                     |
